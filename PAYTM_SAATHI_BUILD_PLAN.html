A complete plan for the Paytm buildathon

From money problem to approved resolution.

Paytm Saathi is an event-first financial resolution layer. A customer explains what happened; Saathi brings together relevant evidence, compares practical paths, and coordinates the chosen next step without taking control away from the customer.

01

Product thesis

The problem statement asks us to make Insurance, Lending, and Fintech simpler, faster, and more human. Saathi starts from a life event rather than asking the customer to choose a product.

Today: product-first

Customers navigate separate insurer, lender, payment, and support apps, repeat their story, compare unfamiliar choices, and track disconnected case numbers.

Saathi: event-first

One conversation creates a case. The system gathers only relevant information, calculates the real funding gap, and compares options against the customer's situation.

Success: resolution

Measure a completed, understood, consented outcome, not chat volume or a product sale. Saathi coordinates; regulated partners make regulated decisions.

Product promise

The customer never has to know which product solves the problem. Saathi recommends claim, payment support, savings, eligible credit, or human help based on customer outcome, never commission.

02

Application and unique USP

This is not another finance chatbot or a product-discovery page. It is a financial resolution workspace with a persistent case, evidence, consent, partner actions, and a status timeline.

USP: The Resolution Passport. Tell Saathi once; it turns the explanation and verified documents into a reusable, source-linked case packet. Each approved insurer, payment, lending, or human-support step reuses the same case facts instead of making the family tell the story again.

Exact-gap funding

Calculate verified coverage and the customer's contribution before considering credit. In the demo: INR 80,000 bill - INR 55,000 policy estimate - INR 10,000 available = INR 15,000 gap.

Resolution graph

Compare claim plus payment plan, savings, exact-gap credit, and wait/escalation by time, cost, risk, and effort. Show why the recommendation won.

Trust ledger

Every important fact points to a bill line, policy clause, sample account record, or named calculation. Uncertain evidence becomes a question or a human handoff.

Naming is a product suggestion for the hackathon demo. The deck's six USP pillars remain: event-first, product-neutral, exact-gap credit, Tell It Once, resolution graph, and trust ledger.

03

Demo journeys

Tell

Customer types or speaks: "Papa hospital mein hain. Bill INR 80,000 hai. Insurance hai, ab kya karun?" They may attach a policy and itemized bill.

Understand

Classify hospitalization and urgency. Parse the policy and bill. Surface coverage terms, a room-rent cap, missing discharge document, and source references.

Compare

Deterministic rules estimate coverage, customer contribution, affordability, and exact gap. Score feasible actions and explain trade-offs.

Approve and track

Customer approves a specific prepared action. n8n triggers the simulated partner workflow and updates one case timeline through closure.

Hero journey: hospitalization

INPUT

Voice/chat plus sample hospital bill and policy PDF.

EVIDENCE

Policy clause, bill lines, missing documents, and customer cash context.

DECISION

Claim + 3-month plan for INR 15,000, compared with savings and a full-bill loan.

OUTCOME

Approval-gated simulated claim/funding action and a live status timeline.

Second journey: unrecognized UPI payment

Pick a seeded INR 8,500 transaction, confirm it is unfamiliar, show the merchant/time evidence, prepare a dispute and protective-action checklist, ask for approval before opening the simulated case, then track its status. This proves the same case engine serves a fintech problem without building another product app.

Keep a third journey, EMI shortfall, as a seeded scripted example only if the first two journeys are stable. Do not spread the build across three incomplete flows.

Tool design rules

Scope and schema

Every tool has typed input/output, a case ID, a source or partner label, and a least-privilege scope. Reject unknown fields, cross-case IDs, and amounts outside the approved action.

Read versus write

Reads are case-scoped and consent-aware. Writes are disabled by default; the gateway requires current consent plus a matching approval token before invoking n8n or the partner adapter.

Evidence contract

Return facts with source type, source ID, page/line/clause, confidence, and timestamp. Preserve provenance through the decision and UI.

RAG contract

Retrieve policy clauses, not free-floating summaries. The answer includes clause/page citation. Low confidence, missing evidence, or conflict triggers a question or a human handoff.

08

Authentication, authorization, and trust

This is a credible hackathon authorization model, not production Paytm identity or regulated underwriting. Use synthetic user data and clearly label simulated outcomes.

Authentication

Seeded customer sign-in issues a short-lived signed JWT. FastAPI validates signature, expiry, issuer, subject, and role. The browser stores only what the demo needs; never trust a client-supplied user ID.

Authorization

Check case ownership on every read and write. Use scopes such as case:read, document:upload, consent:manage, and action:approve. Separate customer and support roles.

Consent and approval

Consent is purpose-bound, revocable, and recorded. Approval binds to a case, exact action, partner, and amount; a changed payload requires approval again. The approval is not generic permission to execute anything.

Privacy and audit

Mask PII before LLM calls, keep secrets in environment variables, log tool name/scope/source/decision/approval/status, and avoid writing document contents or keys into logs.

Human escalation

Low evidence confidence, policy conflict, distress, or dispute routes to a human with the Resolution Passport and source trail, avoiding repeated storytelling.

Partner boundary

Saathi prepares and coordinates. Insurers make claim decisions; regulated lenders make credit decisions. No production payments, lending, claims, or sensitive sharing in the demo.

Present privacy and consent as design goals. Do not claim legal or regulatory compliance from a prototype; production review requires Paytm security, legal, and regulated-partner sign-off.

Eight-hour implementation plan

Build the vertical slice first; integrate partners behind adapters as time allows. Every external call needs a working offline fallback.

HOUR 0-1 / SCAFFOLD

React + FastAPI, shared schemas, seeded user/profile, case types, run scripts.

HOUR 1-2 / AUTH + DATA

JWT demo login, case ownership, fixture repository, upload/select sample documents.

HOUR 2-3 / MCP

Gateway policy and sample Insurer, Payments, Hospital, Lender, and Knowledge tools.

HOUR 3-4 / EVIDENCE

Parse bill/policy, Cognee retrieval or local fallback, OpenAI structured extraction, citations in UI.

HOUR 4-5 / DECISIONS

Unit-tested coverage, exact-gap, affordability and option scoring. Add confidence gates.

HOUR 5-6 / WORKFLOW

LangGraph states, consent pause, approval-bound write, audit trail, timeline endpoint.

HOUR 6-7 / UX + PARTNERS

Case workspace, Sarvam voice path, n8n webhook/callback, Mochatrade adapter or labeled fixture.

HOUR 7-8 / DEMO HARDEN

Finish hospitalization flow, add seeded UPI path if stable, test failures, rehearse pitch.

Priority order if time slips

MUSTHospital case end-to-end, source-linked facts, exact-gap math, consent-bound simulated action, timeline.

SHOULDJWT/case authorization, policy RAG citations, n8n callback, second UPI playbook.

COULDSarvam speech, Cognee graph enhancement, Mochatrade live adapter, third EMI playbook.

Do not sacrifice the working main flow to claim more integrations. A clear fixture fallback is better than a partner integration that breaks the demo.

13

Demo story and pitch

Opening line: "Financial apps make customers choose a product. Saathi starts with the problem."
  1. Show the customer speaking in Hinglish and attach a hospital bill and policy.
  2. Show clause-level policy evidence, bill lines, and a missing-document warning.
  3. Show the exact math: INR 80,000 bill - INR 55,000 estimated coverage - INR 10,000 customer contribution = INR 15,000 gap.
  4. Compare claim plus a 3-month INR 15,000 plan, savings, a full INR 80,000 loan, and waiting. Explain the recommended path and affordability assumptions.
  5. Open the Resolution Passport to show one story and source trail reused in the claim packet.
  6. Require explicit customer approval, run the simulated n8n action, then show the same case timeline receiving status updates.
  7. Switch to the seeded UPI case to prove the same engine handles a fintech event.

Customer value

Less paperwork, faster clarity, fewer repeated explanations, and safer borrowing.

Paytm value

More completed journeys and relevant cross-vertical assistance without pushy product ranking.

Partner value

Better-prepared claims and cases, cleaner handoffs, and fewer avoidable support loops.

Deck targets such as under two minutes to an action plan, 30% fewer incomplete claims, and 20% fewer repeat contacts are proposed pilot targets, not measured results. Present them as goals, not proven impact.

14

Review checklist before build

  • Hospitalization is the main demo; failed UPI is the second.
  • All demo financial data is synthetic and visibly labeled.
  • Mochatrade's actual integration role is confirmed before claiming a live connection.
  • OpenAI and Sarvam keys are stored locally, never in source control or chat.
  • Every displayed financial fact has a source or is marked as an assumption.
  • Coverage, exact gap, affordability, and ranking are deterministic and tested.
  • Every write requires a fresh, case-specific customer approval.
  • Partner claim/credit decisions and settlement are shown as simulated.
  • Failed provider calls have an explicit fallback and visible status.
  • Security/compliance is described as prototype design, not production certification.
